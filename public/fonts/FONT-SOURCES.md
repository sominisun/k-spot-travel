# Font sources and licenses

The Korea Lens pilot self-hosts the following webfont files.

- Paperlogy 6 SemiBold and 8 ExtraBold: WOFF2 files converted without outline
  changes from the TTF files in the
  [official Paperlogy v1.001 release](https://freesentation.blog/paperlogyfont).
- Pretendard Regular, SemiBold and Bold: subset WOFF2 files from the
  [official Pretendard repository](https://github.com/orioncactus/pretendard).

Both families are distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The
corresponding license texts are stored in `licenses/` and must remain with any
redistribution of the font software. The font files may not be sold by
themselves.
